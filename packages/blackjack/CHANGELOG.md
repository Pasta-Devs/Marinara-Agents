## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-22
- New Conversation game: play Blackjack against the house with up to six characters, from /blackjack or the games picker.
- Pick a character to deal and call each round in their own voice, or let the house deal silently.
- Hit, stand, double down, and split a pair once. Blackjack pays 3 to 2, and the dealer can hit or stand on soft 17.
- Set the starting chips, minimum bet, decks in the shoe, and an optional round limit.
