# Saucelings release notes

## 0.22.1 — 2026-10-27

- On the large Home widget, the caretaker no longer towers over the card: both figures are sized to the card's width.
- Feed, Play, Rest and Cuddle (or Warm, Talk and Listen for an egg) are behind one "Care" button in the bottom-right corner. Tap it and the buttons open to its left; tap it again, or press Escape, to put them away. The button glows when a care would help.

## 0.22.0 — 2026-10-27

- The Nook's top bar holds one "⋯" button: Rename, Background and Tour are inside it. "Name it" stays in view until your sauceling has a name.
- Ask is one line until you open it, under Check in.
- When your caretaker has offered an evolution, the reason is written beside the Evolve button instead of behind a "?".
- Settings are grouped under Visits, Writing and Pictures, with shorter notes and a narrower column on a wide screen. On a phone, pickers take the full width instead of being cut off.
- A caretaker with a long name no longer pushes the buttons in About off the edge of a phone screen.

## 0.21.5 — 2026-10-27

- On a narrow screen, the number boxes in Settings no longer shrink until the value is cut off.

## 0.21.4 — 2026-10-26

- The Journal says how many entries are new since you last looked.
- A pet whose Journal is empty now says so, instead of saying there is no sauceling.
- The meters in the Nook jump to their new level when reduced effects or reduced motion is on, instead of sliding.

## 0.21.3 — 2026-10-26

- On the Home widgets, a meter that needs care now shows its amber fill, and the waiting badge on a care button has its colour back.

## 0.21.2 — 2026-10-26

- The large Nook widget shows the caretaker's latest line under the stage, with the AI label when a model wrote it. The small widget carries the label too.
- The widgets open from a real button, so a keyboard or screen reader can reach them. A resting care button on the large widget says when it's ready again instead of doing nothing.
- Errors, the delete confirmations and the AI label follow your Engine theme's colours, and the Journal uses the same AI label as the rest.

## 0.21.1 — 2026-10-26

- The Menagerie explains what comes to it, shows a dot on its tab when a character finds an egg, and picks the finder to look after a found egg.
- Lock stage has a help tip. Replaying the tour on a hatched sauceling starts with a step about it.
- The Journal groups lines by day; with no sauceling it offers a button to the Nook.
- The Album says how to paint the first picture and what a keepsake is. Hiding "Add instructions" keeps what you typed.
- Rename is spelled out, the care window setting reads more plainly, and with no characters the Nook says who looks after the egg.
- Saved backgrounds say they're shared by all your saucelings, and their delete button is easier to tap.

## 0.21.0 — 2026-10-26

- Easier first steps. Mari greets you in an empty nook and gives a short tour after your first egg; the Tour button replays it.
- Tap a meter's name to see what it does. "Growing paused" now shows under Hunger, and an egg says how it hatches.
- A resting care button, and Bring back in the Menagerie, now say why they can't be used yet.
- About is split into Caretaker, Looks and New egg. Retiring and handing over to another caretaker ask first.
- Settings keeps the image options together and shows a Saved tick. Errors read as plain sentences, with the details a tap away.
- The voice offer comes after the egg arrives, and Esc skips it instead of cancelling the egg. Special saucelings fold away until you want them.

## 0.20.0 — 2026-10-25

- First beta of Saucelings, a pet that lives in Home → Saucelings and on the Home widget grid.
- Pick one of your characters to look after an egg. They care for it on their own schedule and talk in their own voice, drawn from their card, and you cover the gaps they miss.
- How the two of you care for it shapes its temperament. Retire elders to the Menagerie and bring them back whenever you like.
- With your image connection: pixel-art paintings for the Album, a figure of your caretaker for the Nook, and backgrounds of your own.
- Characters you chat with may now and then find an egg of a creature they invent. Not every egg is an ordinary one.
- Install it, restart Marinara Engine when prompted, then open Home → Saucelings.
