## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-14
- New Conversation game: a daily Wordle for you and up to seven characters, from /wordle or the games picker.
- Each chat has its own word of the day, and the day rolls over at your local midnight.
- Everyone guesses the same word on their own board, one guess per turn. The table sees each player's colors, never their letters, and the fewest guesses wins.
- Each seat gets one go a day. Today's results and daily streaks are kept per chat, and characters can talk about how their own game went.
