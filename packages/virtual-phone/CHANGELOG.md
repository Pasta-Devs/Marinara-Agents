# Virtual Phone release notes

## 2.10.2 — 2026-11-08

- New Phones tab on Home. Pick any character, then create or open their own phone without a chat. The phone uses the same device and apps.
- A Home phone is saved apart from the phones in your chats. Nothing you do on it is written to a story, and it does not appear in a chat's phone list.
- Story actions such as Show and Context are not on a Home phone. Apps that still need a chat say so instead of failing quietly.

## 2.10.1 — 2026-10-28

- New Calls app with recents and contacts. Live calls stay unavailable until the phone can hand off to Conversation Calls.
- Bazaar replaces Marketplace and Shop replaces Azmoron. Listings, seller chats, orders and deliveries stay on the phone and settle through Banking.
- The match-three Game app replaces PastaSafari. Your score stays on the phone; only the act of playing can reach the story.
- Show an app screen to the people in the scene. Only the characters who can see the screen learn what is on it, and access to another character's phone ends when you put it down.
- Characters can text a contact, change their phone's power, ringer or connection, write a private note, or post to Noodle from their own phone during a reply.
- Needs a Marinara Engine release after 2.5.0. On older Engines, the version you already have keeps working.
