# Meanwhile release notes

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-05

- First slice. Home -> Meanwhile lists your Circles. A Circle has a cast, a setting, linked chats, a pace and a model.
- Each Circle has its own clock. Run it at x1 or x24, pause it, enter a custom speed, or set the date and time.
- A Circle needs a model before it can run. Until you choose one, the card says "Choose a model to start".
- A timer wakes every 5 minutes. A Circle catches up when you open the tab, when a linked chat has new messages, or every 6 hours if you turn on the background switch.
- Each run fills the days with what the characters do, from their week schedules. A chat schedule wins over the card schedule. Characters without one get a simple day.
- Each Circle day also gets 2 to 5 planned meetings and moments. Home > Meanwhile > Timeline shows what each person does by day, what they do now, and today’s plan.
- Runs do not call a model yet. The plan is for the next slice.
