## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-23
- First release, staging only: a modern life sim in its own Home tab.
- A town on a clock with places, opening hours, day and night, and four seasons of thirty days.
- A job, rent, needs and skills; a home with furniture, cooking and a closet; shops, a mall and online orders.
- A cast from your character cards: their weeks, archetypes and gift tastes read by the model, three bonds each, tier events, relationship steps and memories.
- Scenes told by a visual-novel narrator; sleep, knocking on doors and things to do together.
- Alpha: a save from before a change it depends on warns when opened.
